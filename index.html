How it works

Three steps, no sign-up.

  1. 1

    Save it

    Search an album or artist, or paste the Spotify, Apple Music or Bandcamp link someone sent you.

  2. 2

    Note who told you

    Add the person and what they said. “Track 3 is the one.” It stays with the album.

  3. 3

    Listen when you're ready

    A daily pick, Pick for me with a time limit, and one tap to open it in your music app.

Features

Built around the people who tell you about music.

Every album keeps its story

Who recommended it, what they said, your verdict and notes. Plus genres, a short description from Wikipedia, the track list and length, filled in automatically.

Suggestions with a reason

Suggest an album finds records by artists similar to the ones you save, using open listening data from ListenBrainz. Each one tells you why: Because you loved Hunky Dory.

See whose taste matches yours

The People page collects everyone who recommends you music and shows how often their picks land. Find out it's always your dad.

Your listening, over time

Insights charts what you add and hear, how long picks usually wait, and how much music is still on your list. All worked out on your device.

Listen soon

Star an album to put it on your Listen soon shelf.

Tags

Road trip, focus, rainy day. Filter your list in one tap.

Pick for me

"I have 45 minutes." Earmark finds something that fits.

Dark mode

Light, dark, or match your device. Covers or a compact list.

Export any time

A plain file you can import anywhere, or a spreadsheet.

Keyboard friendly

/ to search, P to pick, S to suggest.

On your phone

Made for the moment someone mentions an album.

Open Earmark in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It opens full screen, works offline, and there's nothing to install from an app store.

Privacy

No account. No server. No tracking.

  • Your list lives in your browser and never leaves your device.
  • Album searches go to MusicBrainz, the open music encyclopedia.
  • Suggestions and descriptions are optional and send only artist or album IDs, never your list, notes or names.
  • Export your whole list as a file whenever you like.
